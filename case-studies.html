CMS & Web Platforms

Umbraco CMS Implementation & Upgrade

We implemented Umbraco CMS for our client’s website and upgraded it to a current, supported Umbraco release, giving the content team an easier editor and the business a secure, modern .NET platform.

Plan your Umbraco upgrade
Platform
Umbraco CMS on .NET
Scope
New implementation and version upgrade
Focus
Editor experience, security and SEO-safe migration

The challenge

Older Umbraco versions stop getting security fixes once they leave support, and moving to a current version is more than a package update: newer Umbraco runs on modern .NET and has a rebuilt back office. The client needed to make that move without losing content, URLs or search visibility.

What we did

  • Umbraco installation and configuration
  • Document types and a clear content model
  • Reusable content blocks for editors
  • Content and media migrated to the new version
  • Custom code and packages updated or replaced
  • 301 redirects for any changed URLs

The outcome

The website runs on a supported Umbraco release with its content and media carried over, and editors build and update pages from ready-made blocks without waiting for a developer.

Our approach

From Legacy to Latest: The Umbraco Upgrade Path

The four steps we follow to move an Umbraco website to a supported version, without losing content or search visibility.

  1. 01

    Audit

    Review the current Umbraco version, packages, custom code, content and hosting to map what has to change.

  2. 02

    Plan

    Pick the target Umbraco version, list the breaking changes and agree the migration and rollback plan.

  3. 03

    Upgrade & migrate

    Move the code to modern .NET, replace unsupported packages, then migrate content and media.

  4. 04

    Test & go live

    Test pages, forms and editor workflows, set up redirects, then switch over and monitor.

  • Content & media migration
  • ASP.NET Core / .NET
  • Content modelling
  • Block List & Block Grid editors
  • Package & custom code updates
  • SEO-safe URL redirects

Eyecare & Vision Health

Marketing Automation with Braze & Salesforce

We connected Salesforce customer data to Braze so an eyecare and vision health business could send timely, personalised messages automatically, from eye test reminders to repeat purchases, without manual campaign work.

Automate your customer journeys
Industry
Eyecare & Vision Health
Platforms
Braze and Salesforce
Scope
CRM data integration and automated customer journeys

The challenge

Customer data lived in Salesforce, but marketing messages were planned and sent by hand. Reminders went out late or not at all, and messages couldn’t easily reflect each customer’s history or preferences.

What we did

  • Braze and Salesforce integration
  • Customer data and consent synced to Braze
  • Audience segments for targeted messages
  • Automated, trigger-based customer journeys
  • Personalised message templates
  • Campaign reporting for the marketing team

The outcome

Customer messages now go out automatically at the right moment, based on up-to-date Salesforce data, and the marketing team spends its time on strategy instead of sending campaigns by hand.

How it works

From CRM Data to the Right Message

How customer data flows from Salesforce through Braze to a personalised message, automatically.

  2. Data sync

    Profiles, events and preferences kept up to date in Braze.

    Segments and triggers decide who gets which message, and when.

Case Study FAQs

Can Maven Technosoft build a flight booking system for my airline or OTA?

Yes. We build flight ticket booking systems and internet booking engines for airlines, travel companies and OTAs, covering flight search, fares, seat selection, payment, refunds and booking changes.

Do you build airline mobile app features such as boarding passes and flight tracking?

Yes. Our airline work includes a boarding pass wallet and live airplane tracking for a mobile app.

Can you automate airline refunds?

Yes. We implemented a full refund flow process as part of an airline booking platform.

Can you improve our existing booking process?

Yes. We revamped the booking process for an airline client, and we can review your current booking flow and recommend changes.

Do you build airline operations systems such as crew and office management?

Yes. For an airline client we built crew management and office management systems that cover the airline’s entire ecosystem, alongside its booking platform and mobile app.

Can you integrate an airline with external carriers?

Yes. We delivered an external carrier integration for an airline, connecting its systems with partner carriers so data flows between them through one integration layer.

Can you upgrade an old Umbraco website to the latest version?

Yes. We audit your current Umbraco version, packages and custom code, then upgrade the site to a current, supported Umbraco release on modern .NET and migrate your content and media.

Do you build new websites on Umbraco CMS?

Yes. We implement Umbraco from scratch, including the content model, page templates and reusable blocks, so your team can create and update pages without a developer.

Will we lose content or search rankings during an Umbraco upgrade?

We plan every upgrade to carry over your content, media and URLs, and we add 301 redirects wherever a URL has to change, so search engines and visitors still find every page.

Can you integrate Braze with Salesforce?

Yes. We connect Salesforce to Braze so customer profiles, events and consent stay in sync, then use that data to power segments and automated customer journeys in Braze.

What marketing can an eyecare or optical business automate?

Common examples are eye test recall reminders, appointment confirmations, contact lens reorder reminders, welcome series for new customers and personalised offers, each triggered by customer data.

How do you handle customer consent and privacy in marketing automation?

We sync each customer’s consent and channel preferences into Braze, message only people who have opted in, and keep sensitive health details out of marketing messages.
