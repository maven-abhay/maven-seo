Airlines & Travel

Flight Ticket Booking System for an Airline

An end-to-end flight booking platform that lets travellers search, book and pay for flights on the web and in the mobile app, then manage refunds, boarding passes and flight status in one place.

Discuss a similar project
Industry
Airlines & Travel
Platforms
Web booking engine and mobile app
Scope
Booking, refunds, boarding passes and live flight tracking

The challenge

The client needed a fast, reliable way to sell flight tickets directly to travellers, with live fares and availability across every channel.

What we built

  • Flight search with fare display and filters
  • Seat selection and ancillary add-ons
  • GDS and NDC fare integration
  • Secure payment and e-ticket issue
  • Booking changes and cancellations

The outcome

Travellers can book flights in a few steps on any device, and the client’s team manages fares, bookings and changes from a single back office.

Airline Software FAQs

Can Maven Technosoft build a flight booking system for my airline or OTA?

Yes. We build flight ticket booking systems and internet booking engines for airlines, travel companies and OTAs, covering flight search, fares, seat selection, payment, refunds and booking changes.

Do you build airline mobile app features such as boarding passes and flight tracking?

Yes. Our airline work includes a boarding pass wallet and live airplane tracking for a mobile app.

Can you automate airline refunds?

Yes. We implemented a full refund flow process as part of an airline booking platform.

Can you improve our existing booking process?

Yes. We revamped the booking process for an airline client, and we can review your current booking flow and recommend changes.
