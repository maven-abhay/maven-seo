About Maven

Your Right Technology & AI Partner for high-transaction, domain-heavy businesses.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about engaging Maven Technosoft for IT, data and AI work.

  • Which industries does Maven Technosoft specialize in?

    Our primary verticals are Travel, Transportation, Hospitality and Logistics (TTHL), and Healthcare. We also serve enterprise clients with cross-cutting needs in cloud, data and AI.

  • Do you build AI products end-to-end, or only consult?

    Both. We help with AI strategy, opportunity assessment and roadmaps, and we also build, deploy and operate generative-AI, ML and computer-vision solutions in production — including LLM apps, RAG pipelines, predictive models and AI agents integrated with your core systems.

  • How do you handle data privacy and security for AI projects?

    We design AI systems to keep customer data inside your perimeter — using VPC and on-prem deployments, PII redaction, role-based access, audit trails and model evaluations. Our engineers are familiar with security and regulatory expectations across travel, logistics, hospitality and healthcare.

  • What engagement models do you offer?

    Fixed-scope projects, T&M engagements, dedicated offshore teams, managed services and AI proofs-of-value. We’ll recommend the model that fits your maturity, budget and timeline best.

  • Where is Maven Technosoft based and where do you deliver from?

    Maven Technosoft is headquartered in Noida, India and serves global clients with onshore, offshore and hybrid delivery models.
