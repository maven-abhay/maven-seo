IBE & booking engines

Internet booking engines for airline and travel websites and apps: search, fare display, seat selection and payment.

GDS & NDC integration

Connections to global distribution systems and IATA NDC APIs, so you can sell fares and rich content in every channel.

Ancillaries & loyalty

Systems to sell bags, seats, meals and bundles, and to run frequent-flyer and loyalty programmes.

Revenue management

Pricing and revenue analytics that help you forecast demand and set the right fare for every seat.

AI traveller copilots

AI assistants that answer traveller questions, help with bookings and changes, and hand over to an agent when needed.

Why industry focus makes better software

Generic software partners learn your business on your budget. Because we focus on a small number of industries, our teams already understand the processes, systems and regulations involved, from airline fares and NDC to fleet tracking and optical retail.

That focus means shorter discovery, fewer surprises during integration and solutions that fit how your people actually work. It also means we bring ideas from projects in similar businesses, not just from a requirements document.

Built around how your business runs

Each industry we serve has its own systems and pressures. Airlines depend on distribution systems, fares and servicing; logistics companies on fleets, warehouses and delivery promises; and healthcare businesses on appointments, recalls and optical retail. We design software, data platforms and AI around those realities.

Whichever industry you are in, the same services apply: web and mobile apps, application maintenance, cloud and DevOps, data and analytics, AI and UI/UX and CMS. What changes is the domain knowledge we bring to them.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which industries does Maven Technosoft serve?

Airlines and travel, healthcare, transportation and logistics, and hospitality and OTAs.

Do you build software for airlines?

Yes. We build booking engines, GDS and NDC integrations, refund automation, ancillary and loyalty systems, mobile apps and AI assistants for airlines. See our airline software page.

Do you have experience in healthcare?

Yes. We delivered marketing automation with Braze and Salesforce for a healthcare brand.

Can you work in an industry that is not listed?

Our deepest experience is in the industries above, but our engineering, data and AI services apply to many high-transaction businesses. Talk to us about your needs.

Do you build software for logistics companies?

Yes. We build asset management, fleet tracking, warehouse and yard automation, predictive ETAs, route optimisation and control-tower analytics for transportation and logistics businesses.

What do you build for hospitality businesses and OTAs?

Booking engines, channel managers, complete back-office suites for OTAs, dynamic pricing and AI-driven personalisation.
