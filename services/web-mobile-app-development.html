Web & Mobile App Development

High-performance web and mobile applications with back-end system integration, powered by rigorous testing, modern UI/UX and resilient APIs, built for high-transaction businesses.

Discuss your app

Apps that hold up when transactions matter

In travel, logistics and financial services, an app is rarely a stand-alone product. It has to talk to booking systems, fleet platforms, payment providers, CRMs and legacy back ends, and it has to keep working when traffic peaks. We build web and mobile applications with that reality in mind.

Our teams combine product design, front-end and mobile engineering, API development and quality assurance. We start from the outcome you need, such as more direct bookings, fewer support calls or faster operations, and shape the app around it.

Recent work includes a flight booking platform with a mobile boarding pass wallet and live flight tracking, and Umbraco CMS websites for content-heavy brands. See our case studies.

Why choose Maven for app development

We are a focused team with deep experience in high-transaction industries. That means we already understand the systems your app must connect to, such as booking engines, GDS and NDC, CRMs, payment gateways and logistics platforms, and the peaks your app must survive.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you build both web and mobile apps?

Yes. We build web applications, iOS and Android apps and the APIs and back-end integrations behind them.

Can you integrate a new app with our existing systems?

Yes. Integration is a core part of our work, including booking systems, GDS and NDC, CRM, ERP and payment gateways.

Can you take over and improve an app another team built?

Yes. We start with a code and architecture review, fix the most urgent issues and then plan improvements with you.

Which industries do you build apps for?

Mainly airlines and travel, transportation and logistics, hospitality, eyecare and vision health, and banking and financial services.

Should we build a native or a cross-platform mobile app?

It depends on your users, features and budget. Native apps give the best performance and device access; cross-platform apps share one code base across iOS and Android. We recommend the right option after discovery.

Do you also design the app?

Yes. Our UI/UX team handles research, prototypes and interface design, so design and development work as one team.

Can you build a minimum viable product (MVP) first?

Yes. We can start with an MVP that covers the most important journeys, launch it to real users and grow it based on their feedback.

How do you test web and mobile apps?

We combine automated tests with manual testing on real devices and browsers, and run performance and security checks before each release.

Can you publish our app to the App Store and Google Play?

Yes. We prepare the store listings and builds, handle the review process and set up release management for future updates.

Can you add features to an app that is already live?

Yes. We regularly extend live apps with new journeys and integrations. For example, we added a refund flow, a boarding pass wallet and live flight tracking to an airline booking platform and mobile app.
