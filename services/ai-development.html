AI that moves real business numbers

Many AI projects stop at an impressive demo. We focus on the work after the demo: connecting models to your data and systems, measuring quality, controlling cost and risk, and running the solution reliably for real users.

Our AI work spans generative AI and large language model (LLM) applications, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) over your own documents, predictive and prescriptive machine learning, computer vision and AI agents that automate multi-step workflows. Each solution is measured against business targets agreed with you, such as resolution time, forecast accuracy or hours saved.

We apply it where we know the domain best: travel copilots and revenue management for airlines and OTAs, ETA and capacity models for logistics, and fraud and risk scoring for banking. See the full range on our AI solutions page.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which AI models do you work with?

We build on GPT, Claude, Gemini and open-source large language models, and choose the model for each use case based on quality, cost, speed and data requirements.

Can our data stay private?

Yes. We can deploy AI solutions in your own cloud account or on-premises, with access controls and protection for personal data.

What is retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)?

RAG lets an AI model answer questions using your own documents and data. It retrieves the relevant content first, then generates an answer with citations, which makes answers more accurate and traceable.

How long does an AI prototype take?

It depends on how ready your data is and which systems the prototype must connect to. We agree a timeline after a short discovery, and aim to put a working prototype in front of users as early as possible.

How do you stop an AI assistant from giving wrong answers?

We ground answers in your own approved content with citations, add guardrails for topics it must not handle, test it against real questions and hand over to a human when confidence is low.

Can AI connect to our existing systems?

Yes. We connect AI solutions to systems such as booking platforms, GDS, CRM, ERP and ticketing tools through secure APIs.

Do we need a lot of data to start with AI?

Not always. Generative AI solutions can often start with your existing documents and knowledge, while predictive models need historical data. We check data readiness before recommending an approach.

How do you control the running costs of AI?

We choose the right-sized model for each task, cache repeated answers, limit unnecessary calls and monitor usage, so costs stay predictable as adoption grows.
