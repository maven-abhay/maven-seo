Big Data & Analytics Services

Data platforms, lakes, real-time pipelines and BI dashboards engineered for the high-transaction, low-margin reality of travel, logistics and BFSI.

Talk to a data expert

From scattered data to decisions you can trust

Airlines, OTAs, logistics operators and banks produce huge volumes of transactions every day: searches, bookings, shipments, payments and customer interactions. The data is valuable, but it usually sits in separate systems with different formats and definitions.

We build the data foundations that bring it together: reliable pipelines, a well-modelled data platform and dashboards that answer the questions your teams actually ask. Where timing matters, such as pricing, fraud or delivery tracking, we stream data in near real time.

A sound data platform is also the starting point for AI. Clean, connected data is what makes forecasting, personalisation and machine learning work in production.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a data lake and a data warehouse?

A data lake stores large amounts of raw data in many formats at low cost. A data warehouse stores cleaned, structured data that is ready for reporting. Many businesses use both.

Can you build real-time dashboards?

Yes. Where timing matters, we stream data from source systems so dashboards and alerts update in near real time.

Which industries do you build analytics for?

Mainly airlines and travel, transportation and logistics, hospitality and OTAs, and banking and financial services.

How do you protect personal data in analytics?

We apply access control, masking and retention rules to personal data, and agree the approach with your data protection team.

Can you work with the BI tool we already use?

Yes. We build data models and dashboards for the reporting tools you already have, or recommend one if you are starting fresh.

Where should our data platform run?

Usually in your existing cloud, such as AWS, Azure or Google Cloud, so data stays close to your applications and under your control.

How long until we see the first dashboard?

We aim to deliver a first useful dashboard early, built on a small set of trusted sources, then extend it in short releases. The exact timeline depends on your data sources.

Can you combine data from many different systems?

Yes. Most of our data work brings together booking, operations, CRM, finance and partner data into one model with shared definitions, so every team works from the same numbers.

Do you also maintain the data platform after launch?

Yes. We can monitor pipelines and data quality, add new sources and keep dashboards up to date as your business changes.
