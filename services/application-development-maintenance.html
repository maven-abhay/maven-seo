Application Development & Maintenance

End-to-end ADM services: design, build, integrate, reconcile and maintain, including 24×7 support for mission-critical travel and logistics platforms and a steady roadmap of planned improvements.

Talk about support

Keep critical applications running and improving

Most of the value of business software is created after launch. Fares change, regulations change, partners update their APIs and users ask for new features. Application development and maintenance (ADM) keeps your systems reliable while they continue to evolve.

We take responsibility for the full life of an application: new features, integrations, bug fixes, performance tuning, security updates and data reconciliation. For travel and logistics platforms where downtime directly costs revenue, we provide round-the-clock support, with clear escalation paths and regular reports so you always know the health of your applications.

Our Umbraco upgrade project is a good example: we moved a website from an ageing version to a supported one without losing content or search visibility.

ADM Services

New features

24×7 support

Why outsource application maintenance to Maven

Keeping a business-critical application healthy takes steady, specialist attention that is hard to spare from an in-house team focused on new projects. With Maven, you get a team that knows your application, watches it closely and fixes problems before they reach your customers.

Because we also build new software, maintenance with us is not just keeping the lights on. We reduce technical debt, upgrade ageing platforms and deliver improvements on a roadmap agreed with you. Frequently Asked Questions

What does application development and maintenance include?

It covers new features, bug fixes, integrations, upgrades, performance and security work, and day-to-day support for an application after it goes live.

Do you offer 24×7 support?

Yes. We provide 24×7 support for mission-critical travel and logistics platforms.

Can you maintain an application we did not build?

Yes. We start with an assessment and a structured knowledge transfer, then take over support and development.

Can you upgrade an old CMS or framework version?

Yes. For example, we have upgraded an Umbraco CMS website to a supported version, including content migration and redirects.

How do you report on support and maintenance work?

We agree reporting with you at the start, typically covering incidents, fixes, releases, open risks and planned improvements for the period.

Can you modernise a legacy application while supporting it?

Yes. We modernise step by step, replacing or upgrading parts of the application while keeping it running for your users.

Which technologies can you support?

We support the web, mobile, cloud and CMS applications we build, including .NET and Umbraco, and review any other technology during the assessment before taking it on.

Can you reconcile data between our systems?

Yes. Reconciliation is part of our ADM service: we keep bookings, payments, finance and partner data consistent, flag mismatches and fix their causes.

Is application maintenance only for old systems?

No. New applications need maintenance too: security updates, dependency upgrades, performance tuning and small improvements keep them healthy as usage grows.
