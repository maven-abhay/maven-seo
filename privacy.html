This policy explains what personal information Maven Technosoft Pvt. Ltd. ("Maven", "we", "us") collects through maventechno.com, why we collect it and what we do with it.

At a glance

  • No tracking We do not use advertising or analytics cookies.
  • Never sold We do not sell your personal information.
  • Your control Ask to see, correct or delete your data.

What we collect

  • Contact form: your name, email address and message, plus your company, phone number and subject if you give them.
  • Newsletter: your email address.
  • Technical data: your IP address, used for a short time to stop spam and abuse of the forms, and the standard request logs kept by our hosting provider.

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies, and we do not track you across other websites.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and discuss a possible project with you.
  • To send the newsletter you signed up for. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To keep the website secure and prevent spam.

We do not sell your personal information or share it for marketing.

Where it goes

Form submissions are delivered as email to our Microsoft 365 mailbox. The website is hosted on Microsoft Azure. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser sends your IP address to Google when the page loads. These providers process data on our behalf, and some of them may process it outside India.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for as long as we need them to respond and to keep normal business records. Newsletter addresses are kept until you unsubscribe. IP addresses used for spam protection are discarded within an hour.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it. Email info@maventechno.com and we'll respond within 30 days.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
