About Maven

Your Right Technology & AI Partner for high-transaction, domain-heavy businesses.

Maven Technosoft Pvt. Ltd. is a privately held professional IT products and services company, headquartered in Noida, India. Founded by domain experts from the IT industry, our prime focus is on TTHL (Travel, Transportation, Hospitality and Logistics) and Eyecare & Vision Health — verticals where every transaction, every margin and every customer experience matters.

Our Mandate

Deliver world-class, domain-driven IT & AI services to global clients in our chosen verticals — with the flexibility, efficiency and accountability of a focused boutique partner.

Built for Industry

AI-Native Engineering

Our story

Maven Technosoft was founded in 2017 by domain experts from the IT industry who saw that high-transaction businesses were poorly served by generalist IT vendors. Airlines, travel companies, hotels and logistics operators needed partners who understood their processes as well as their technology.

Today our team of more than 50 technology and domain experts has delivered over 500 projects for clients in the United States, Canada, South America, Europe, the Middle East and Asia Pacific. We have grown by staying focused: fewer industries, deeper knowledge and long-term relationships with the businesses we serve.
