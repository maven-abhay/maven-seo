One partner from first idea to long-term support

Most of our clients run high-transaction businesses where every booking, shipment or payment counts. They need more than a supplier who writes code: they need a partner who understands the domain, connects new software to the systems already in place and keeps it running after launch.

That is why our services are designed to work together. A booking engine needs cloud infrastructure that copes with peak demand, data that shows what travellers do and, increasingly, AI that helps them do it faster. You can start with one service and add others as you grow.

Frequently Asked Questions

What IT services does Maven Technosoft provide?

Airline booking engine development, web and mobile app development, application development and maintenance, cloud and DevOps, big data and analytics, AI and machine learning, and UI/UX and CMS development.

Can we start with a small project?

Yes. Many clients start with a focused project, such as a single module, an upgrade or a prototype, and expand from there.

Do you support applications after launch?

Yes. We offer ongoing support and maintenance, including 24×7 support for mission-critical travel and logistics platforms.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. We work with clients in North America, South America, Europe, the Middle East and Asia Pacific.

Which industries are your services designed for?

Mainly airlines and travel, transportation and logistics, hospitality and OTAs, and eyecare and vision health.

Can you integrate new software with our existing systems?

Yes. Integration with booking systems, GDS and NDC, CRM, ERP, payment gateways and partner APIs is part of almost every project we deliver.

How much does a software project cost?

It depends on the scope, the integrations involved and the timeline. After a short discovery we give you a clear estimate and a delivery plan, so you know the cost before work starts.

How do you keep projects on track?

We work in short cycles with regular demos, a visible backlog and progress reports, and we raise risks early so decisions can be made in time.

What happens after the project goes live?

We can stay on to support, maintain and improve the solution, or hand it over to your team with documentation and a walkthrough.
