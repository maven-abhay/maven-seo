Travel technology is our home ground

Airline and travel retail is one of the most demanding environments for software. Prices change by the minute, inventory comes from many sources, margins are thin and travellers expect a smooth experience from search to arrival. Maven Technosoft was founded by domain experts from the IT industry, and travel, transportation, hospitality and logistics have been our core focus from the start.

We help airlines, online travel agencies and travel companies sell more directly, automate costly manual work and keep travellers informed. That covers the customer-facing booking engine and app, the integrations with distribution systems and payment providers, and the back-office tools your teams use every day.

Our recent work for an airline includes a flight ticket booking system, a full refund flow, a mobile boarding pass wallet, live airplane tracking and a booking process revamp, plus crew management and office management for the airline’s entire ecosystem and an external carrier integration.

Frequently Asked Questions

What airline software does Maven Technosoft build?

We build booking engines, GDS and NDC integrations, refund and change automation, ancillary and loyalty systems, airline mobile apps, revenue analytics, AI traveller assistants and back-office tools for OTAs.

Have you worked with an airline before?

Yes. We delivered a flight ticket booking system for an airline, including a full refund flow, a mobile boarding pass wallet, live airplane tracking, a booking process revamp, crew and office management for the entire ecosystem, and an external carrier integration.

What is NDC and why does it matter?

NDC (New Distribution Capability) is an IATA standard that lets airlines sell richer offers, such as bundles and ancillaries, through modern APIs instead of only through traditional GDS channels.

Do you work with OTAs and travel management companies?

Yes. We build booking engines, back-office suites and integrations for online travel agencies and travel companies as well as airlines.

Can you use AI in airline customer service?

Yes. We build AI assistants that answer traveller questions, help with bookings and changes, and hand over to a human agent when needed.

Can you integrate with our existing passenger service system?

Yes. We plan integrations with your existing reservation, departure control and back-office systems during discovery, and build the booking and servicing features around them.
