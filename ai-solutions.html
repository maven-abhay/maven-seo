Frequently Asked Questions

What AI solutions does Maven Technosoft build?

Generative AI and LLM applications, RAG and knowledge search, predictive and prescriptive machine learning, computer vision, intelligent automation and AI governance.

Which AI models do you use?

We build on GPT, Claude, Gemini and open-source large language models, and choose the model for each use case based on quality, cost, speed and data requirements.

Can AI run inside our own cloud or data centre?

Yes. We offer secure deployment in your own cloud account (VPC), with access controls and protection for personal data.

How do you make sure AI answers are accurate and safe?

We evaluate solutions against test sets, add guardrails, ground answers in your own data with citations and monitor quality after launch.

Where should we start with AI?

Start with one use case that has clear value, available data and a measurable target. We help you pick it, build a prototype on your own data and decide whether to scale.

Do you provide AI governance and compliance support?

Yes. We help with AI strategy, governance, evaluations, red-teaming, privacy and PII protection, and model monitoring for regulated industries.

What is the difference between generative AI and predictive machine learning?

Generative AI creates content such as answers, summaries or drafts from your data. Predictive machine learning forecasts numbers or outcomes, such as demand, ETAs or prices. Many solutions use both.

Can AI help our customer support team?

Yes. AI assistants can answer common questions, help customers with bookings and changes, summarise cases for agents and hand over to a person when needed.
